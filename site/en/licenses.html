Names, icons and screenshots

The Clean Notch app icon and Muse / Grok Bot pixel graphics are project design resources. Client names describe compatibility and do not imply authorization, certification or endorsement. Third-party trademarks belong to their respective owners; Apache 2.0 does not grant trademark rights.

Product screenshots show the running Mac Island app with clearly labeled demonstration data. They do not show private user conversations.

Contributing

Track changes through issues and pull requests. Read the contribution guide, use synthetic test data and keep user information and machine-specific configuration out of commits.
